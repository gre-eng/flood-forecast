This tool looks at river levels and typical seasonal rainfall, month by month. It cannot see sudden flash floods, blocked drains in towns and cities, or coastal and tidal flooding. If it is raining heavily where you are, take care even when the risk shows as low.

Forecasts combine live river-flow data where reliable, and a historical rainfall-and-flooding model elsewhere. Safety guidance is sourced from NEMA public advisories.
